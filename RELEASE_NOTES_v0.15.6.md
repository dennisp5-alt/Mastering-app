- Stereo 48 kHz sources with unusually strong isolated peaks, reliable broad mastering demand and no previous-master/lossy guard may trial the v0.15.5 verified sparse-peak candidate **before** rendering specialist candidates.
- All WAV verification, mastering-delta, passage-preservation, true-peak, loudness, objective and full Studio delivery gates are unchanged; a failed audition resumes the original v0.15.5 competition.
- When the early candidate passes all gates, redundant alternative candidate renders are bypassed. This is a narrower competition, *not* proof it always selects the best option among every possible process on every song. Song-specific listening and comparison remain required.
- Previously mastered, stem-assisted, non-peak-constrained or low-confidence source material uses the original candidate competition. Existing successful masters should be checked with SHA and level-matched audio before claiming bit-for-bit identity.
- QC identifies the shortcut, its decision and `early_sparse_peak_audition_ms` timing. There is no GPU processing or lower-resolution rendering.
- Goal: lower runtime and battery demand for sparse-peak winners while preserving v0.15.5's proven render sound. Real-device runtime is unverified at build time.
